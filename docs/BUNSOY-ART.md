# Bunsoy artwork

Bunsoy uses the original illustrated Shih Tzu in `src/Pet.jsx`: white fur, brown patches near the eye, on the ear and flank, and a blue bandana. Preserve this original artwork; the dog is not claymorphic.

The pearl-and-rose claymorphism in `src/clay.css` applies to the app interface, including the companion's controls and speech bubble. Today, Settings, check-ins, and the desktop companion all share the original dog component and its head, tail, and eye animations. Reduced motion remains supported.

The earlier generated `assets/bunsoy.png` is unused. Companion naming and saved study records are unchanged by the artwork restoration.
